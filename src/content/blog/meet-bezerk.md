Bezerk is a small sculpture inspired by the African rain frog—that famously grumpy, impossibly round amphibian. In real life those frogs puff up and look like they have opinions. Bezerk has opinions too, mostly about whether your hiding spot is *cheerful enough*.

He is 3D printed, sanded, and left paint-ready. Acrylics, enamels, markers: if it sticks to plastic, it is fair game. Some people give him a full makeover; others leave him creamy white so he blends into laundry piles (rude but effective).

The magnets live in his feet. Table legs, fridge doors, metal shelves, the lip of a toolbox—anywhere steel says hello. He is not heavy; he is persistent.

When someone finds him, they can take him home, add a coat of paint, and tuck him somewhere new. Then the cycle repeats. That is the whole bezerk economy: find, delight, re-hide.
