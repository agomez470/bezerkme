A good bezerk hide is **visible if you are looking**, but easy to miss if you are not. Think: eye level surprises, not buried treasure.

**Happy places that work**

- The side of a desk leg (magnets!)
- Behind a plant pot, peeking one warty shoulder out
- On a metal shelf with books pretending he is literature
- Inside a mug cupboard, facing outward like he lives there now

**Places to reconsider**

- Ovens (he is plastic)
- Direct sun for weeks (paint fades; frog sulks)
- Somewhere that will get wet (bathrooms need careful placement)

Leave a tiny clue if the finder is young or impatient: a note, an arrow, or bezerk “accidentally” facing the hallway. The joy is in the *almost* missing him.

When someone spots him, let them hold him, spin him, decide on a new color if they want. Then choose the next hide together. Bezerk approves of teamwork.
