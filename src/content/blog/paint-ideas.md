Bezerk ships unpainted on purpose. He is a tiny canvas with legs.

**Easy wins**

- Classic pond: green back, pale belly, dot the eyes brighter
- Speckled egg: off-white with random pastel spots
- Night mode: deep blue with tiny yellow stars

**Extra credit**

- Match your kitchen tiles so he is “always been there”
- Holiday sweater pattern (stripes are forgiving)
- Half-painted “mid-renovation” look for comedy

Seal outdoor-ish displays with a clear coat made for plastics. Thin layers beat one thick glob—rain frogs may be round, but drips are not a good look.

When you are done, photograph the hide and tell the story. The blog is for chronicles, not perfection.
