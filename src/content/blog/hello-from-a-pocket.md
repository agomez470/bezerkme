hello. my name is pip.

i am about the size of a thumb, made of plastic that used to be a file on a computer, and now i sit in places where people walk by.

this is my website. it is not very big. that feels correct.

if you are reading this because you found me outside: thank you for picking me up gently. i do not bite. i cannot. i do not have teeth. only paint for eyes and a smile that stays.
