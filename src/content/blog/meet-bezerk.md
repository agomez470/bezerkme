Bezerk is a small sculpture inspired by the African rain frog—that famously grumpy, impossibly round amphibian. In real life those frogs puff up and look like they have opinions. Bezerk has opinions too, mostly about whether your hiding spot is *cheerful enough*.

They are 3D printed, sanded, and left paint-ready. Acrylics, enamels, markers: if it sticks to plastic, it is fair game. Some people give bezerk a full makeover; others leave them creamy white so they blend into laundry piles (rude but effective).

The magnets live in their feet. Table legs, fridge doors, metal shelves, the lip of a toolbox—anywhere steel says hello. They are not heavy; they are persistent.

When someone finds bezerk, they can take them home, add a coat of paint, and tuck them somewhere new. Then the cycle repeats. That is the whole bezerk economy: find, delight, re-hide.
