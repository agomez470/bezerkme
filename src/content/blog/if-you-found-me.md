hi finder.

you do not owe me anything. there is no quest, no app, no scoreboard.

a few gentle options:

- leave me where you found me for the next person
- move me somewhere nearby that feels kind
- take a photo if you want
- visit this page again later; i write slowly

please do not put me somewhere dangerous (roads, edges, mouths of curious dogs). i am brave, but i am also three centimeters tall.

thank you for noticing something small.
