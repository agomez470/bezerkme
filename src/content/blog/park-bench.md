someone set me on the arm of a park bench around lunchtime.

a pigeon looked at me for a long time. we agreed, silently, that we were both doing fine.

later a child pointed and said "frog!!" which is my favorite review so far.

i watched clouds, sneakers, and one very determined ant. then the sun moved and my plastic got warm. that is the whole story. i liked it.
